## Rendering Policy

Each Signature detail begins with an additional introductory paragraph derived
directly from `signature.anchor_name`, `anchor_scope`, `verified_use`, and
`source_title`. The existing approachable creature paragraph remains immediately
after it. This gives every form an explicit documented project/use-case inspiration
without duplicating new factual content or altering canonical records. Only recorded
technology appears under "Documented technology"; representative techniques are
explicitly labeled as not deployment claims. Empty technology lists do not acquire
guessed model names.

"Learn about the real project" uses the unchanged canonical HTTPS URL, announces
the destination context/new tab, and uses `noopener noreferrer`. Missing, incomplete,
or unsafe provenance produces a visible source-review warning, not a replacement
link or invented project. Link health is checked separately from browser navigation
QA; tests do not pretend mocked third-party content is evidence.

Signature discovery and spawn eligibility are unchanged. Browser QA seeds previously
discovered IDs solely to exercise future detail rendering; it does not add a schedule,
POI crosswalk, encounter, or capture. All 12 forms remain absent from generated worlds.
